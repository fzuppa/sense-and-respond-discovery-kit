# Complete AI Prompts by Canvas Box
## Product Discovery for Agile Teams

This document organizes all AI prompts (Generation, Selection, and Review) by Lean Product Canvas box for easy reference.

---

## 🎯 Core Principle

**AI outputs are assumptions that must be validated, not facts to be accepted.**

Use these prompts to accelerate your discovery work, but always treat AI-generated content as assumptions requiring validation with real evidence.

---

## How to Use This Document

**Three types of prompts for each Canvas box:**

1. **GENERATION PROMPTS** (Optional)
   - Use when: You need ideas, are stuck, or want alternatives
   - AI does: Generates multiple options
   - You do: Evaluate and treat as assumptions

2. **SELECTION PROMPTS** (After Generation)
   - Use when: AI gave you too many options
   - AI does: Asks you questions about options
   - You do: Choose ONE focus + organize backlog

3. **REVIEW PROMPTS** (Recommended)
   - Use when: After you've completed work manually
   - AI does: Critiques format, clarity, identifies assumptions
   - You do: Revise your own work

# BOX 1: Business Problem Statement

## Required Format

```
[Our service/product] was designed to achieve [these goals]. We have observed 
[in this way] that the product/service isn't meeting these goals which is causing 
[this adverse effect/problem] to our business. How might we improve service/product 
so that our customers are more successful as determined by [these measurable changes 
in their behavior]?
```

---

## GENERATION PROMPT: Business Problem Statement

**When to use:** Reframing solutions as problems, exploring alternative problem statements

```
I'm working on defining a business problem using the Lean Product Canvas. 
Here's my situation:
[DESCRIBE YOUR SITUATION - e.g., "We're an online learning company. People take one 
class and don't come back. Our CEO wants to implement discount codes."]

Help me create Business Problem Statements using this EXACT format:

"[Our service/product] was designed to achieve [these goals]. We have observed 
[in this way] that the product/service isn't meeting these goals which is causing 
[this adverse effect/problem] to our business. How might we improve service/product 
so that our customers are more successful as determined by [these measurable changes 
in their behavior]?"

Please:
1. Generate 2-3 different problem statements using this exact format
2. For each statement, identify what assumptions are being made
3. Note what evidence would validate this is the actual problem
4. Avoid jumping to solutions - stay focused on the problem

Use the exact format above. I need problem statements, not solutions.
```

**Validation Required:** Gather evidence (data, stakeholder input, or research) that tests which problem framing reflects reality.

## SELECTION PROMPT: Business Problem Statement

**When to use:** After Generation Prompt gives you 2-3 problem statement options

```
I've generated multiple business problem statement options and need help selecting 
which one to focus on first. Here are my options:

[PASTE YOUR 2-3 PROBLEM STATEMENT OPTIONS]

I need to choose ONE to focus on and organize the others as a backlog. Please help 
me think through this decision by asking me questions and showing trade-offs. 
Do NOT choose for me.

Guide me through this decision by:

1. EVIDENCE CHECK
   For each option, ask:
   - What evidence do I currently have that supports this is the real problem?
   - What would I need to validate to confirm this problem framing?
   - Which option has the most existing evidence?

2. IMPACT ANALYSIS
   For each option, ask:
   - If we solve this problem, what business impact would we see?
   - Which problem, if solved, creates the most value?
   - Which problem is most urgent vs. most important?

3. FEASIBILITY CHECK
   For each option, ask:
   - Do we have the ability to address this problem?
   - What's in our control vs. outside our control?
   - Which problem can we realistically impact?

4. FOCUS TEST
   Help me consider:
   - Which problem, if I focus on it exclusively for 3 months, would matter most?
   - Which problems might be symptoms of another problem?
   - Which problem is most foundational?

5. DECISION FRAMEWORK
   Present this as a table comparing options across:
   - Existing evidence (High/Medium/Low)
   - Potential business impact (High/Medium/Low)
   - Our ability to address it (High/Medium/Low)
   - Strategic importance (High/Medium/Low)

Then ask ME: "Based on this analysis, which problem statement will you focus on 
first and why?"

After I answer, help me organize the others as a backlog with notes on when to 
revisit each.
```

## REVIEW PROMPT: Business Problem Statement

**When to use:** After you've written your business problem statement

```
I've completed Box 1 (Business Problem Statement) of the Lean Product Canvas. 
Please review my work and provide constructive feedback.

Here is my business problem statement:
[PASTE YOUR COMPLETE PROBLEM STATEMENT]

The required format is:
"[Our service/product] was designed to achieve [these goals]. We have observed 
[in this way] that the product/service isn't meeting these goals which is causing 
[this adverse effect/problem] to our business. How might we improve service/product 
so that our customers are more successful as determined by [these measurable changes 
in their behavior]?"

Please provide feedback on:

1. FORMAT ADHERENCE
   - Does my statement follow the required format?
   - Which sections are missing or incomplete?
   - Are all the template elements present?

2. CLARITY
   - Is the problem clearly articulated?
   - Are the goals specific and understandable?
   - Are the observations concrete or vague?
   - Is the adverse effect measurable/tangible?
   - Are the desired behavior changes specific?

3. RISKY ASSUMPTIONS
   - Identify 2-3 assumptions embedded in this problem statement
   - For each assumption, note why it's risky if wrong
   - Suggest what evidence would test each assumption

Do NOT rewrite my problem statement. Instead, provide specific guidance on how 
I can improve it myself.
```
