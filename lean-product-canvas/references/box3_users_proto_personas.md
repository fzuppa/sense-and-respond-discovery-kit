# Complete AI Prompts by Canvas Box
## Product Discovery for Agile Teams

This document organizes all AI prompts (Generation, Selection, and Review) by Lean Product Canvas box for easy reference.

---

## 🎯 Core Principle

**AI outputs are assumptions that must be validated, not facts to be accepted.**

Use these prompts to accelerate your discovery work, but always treat AI-generated content as assumptions requiring validation with real evidence.

---

## How to Use This Document

**Three types of prompts for each Canvas box:**

1. **GENERATION PROMPTS** (Optional)
   - Use when: You need ideas, are stuck, or want alternatives
   - AI does: Generates multiple options
   - You do: Evaluate and treat as assumptions

2. **SELECTION PROMPTS** (After Generation)
   - Use when: AI gave you too many options
   - AI does: Asks you questions about options
   - You do: Choose ONE focus + organize backlog

3. **REVIEW PROMPTS** (Recommended)
   - Use when: After you've completed work manually
   - AI does: Critiques format, clarity, identifies assumptions
   - You do: Revise your own work

---

# BOX 3: Users (Proto-Personas)

## Purpose

Define the people the product must serve/satisfy. These can be users, customers, or people impacted by the product. Typically expressed as proto-personas.

---

## GENERATION PROMPT: Users (Proto-Personas)

**When to use:** Creating personas, identifying target users

```
I'm creating a persona (Boxes 3-4 on Lean Product Canvas) based on what we know 
about our customers:
[SUMMARIZE YOUR CUSTOMER KNOWLEDGE - insights from interviews, data, observations]

Generate a proto-persona including:
1. Demographics and background
2. Goals related to our product
3. Jobs they're trying to do
4. Pain points and challenges

For each element, indicate:
- Confidence level (high/medium/low) based on evidence provided
- What's validated vs. what's assumed
- What additional research would increase confidence

Be honest about what's speculation vs. what's based on evidence.
```

**Validation Required:** Conduct customer conversations to verify the persona reflects real people, not AI assumptions.

---

## REVIEW PROMPT: Users (Proto-Personas)

**When to use:** After you've created your proto-personas

```
I've completed Box 3 (Users/Proto-Personas) of the Lean Product Canvas. Please 
review my work and provide constructive feedback.

Here is my proto-persona:
[PASTE YOUR PERSONA DESCRIPTION]

Context: Proto-personas define the people our product must serve/satisfy. These 
can be users, customers, or people impacted by the product.

Please provide feedback on:

1. FORMAT ADHERENCE
   - Does this persona include key elements (demographics, role, context)?
   - Is it concise enough for a proto-persona (not overly detailed)?
   - Does it focus on relevant characteristics for this product?

2. CLARITY
   - Is this persona specific enough to guide decisions?
   - Can I visualize a real person from this description?
   - Are the most relevant characteristics emphasized?
   - Does it include behaviors, needs, or goals related to our product?
   - Would different team members interpret this persona the same way?

3. RISKY ASSUMPTIONS
   - Identify 2-3 assumptions made in this persona
   - For each assumption, note what we're assuming about this person
   - Distinguish between: what's validated by research vs. what's assumed
   - Suggest how to test if this persona reflects real users

Do NOT rewrite my persona. Instead, provide specific guidance on how I can 
improve it myself.
```
